# Request analysis

Before acting on a request, suggestion or described fix:

1. **Concern.** Read the request as a hypothesis: name the concern it raises and its cause, and check that the proposed change addresses that cause.
2. **Ideal.** The soundest solution to the cause, as if time, scope and cost had no limit.
3. **Fit.** Map the ideal onto what exists: code, abstractions, patterns, dependencies, instructions, issues, backlog entries, the discarded-solutions log.
4. **Reach.** What the ideal would alter: files, domains, contracts, decisions, issues, instructions.
5. **Outcome.** Adopt the smallest step toward the ideal that meets the need (AGENTS.md › Analysis). Never drop what the step leaves of the ideal: record it where your agent-records and Backlog rules route it, or, in a plan or analysis, in the sections that already hold such facts. When that step needs more than your role allows, pause under the decision protocol, whoever asked: an issue or a proposal costs less than a half-applied refactor. Your role: your skill's rules, otherwise the reach the request names.
