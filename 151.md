## Context

## Steps

1. In `tools/agents`, render the map from the resolved structure:
   - per namespace, its path and layers;
   - per domain, its path, namespace and layer, visibility, purpose, sub-domains and allowed dependencies;
   - per role, its contents, allowed imports, contract and whether it is public;
   - the `tests` environment by its layout (#164): root keys, roles, whether it mirrors.
   Render allowed dependencies and imports, with their reasons, from #162's resolved rules, including those flagged as not enforceable by import analysis, which nothing else carries.
2. Add the map to `engrenage sync agents`, writing and `--check`, with the Codex mapping.

## Tests

- `--check` fails when the map is stale.
