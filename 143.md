# #143 config: layered registry with lazy sections

- State: OPEN
- Labels: 🚀 — Improvement

## Body

Part of #137, step 6. Read the plan, its **Config pipeline** and **`src/` of engrenage** sections, and #146, before starting.

## Context

`createResolvedConfig` (`project/config`, 659 lines of nested closures) builds one aggregate `ResolvedConfig` (`interface extends ReturnType<typeof createResolvedConfig>`) and imports `tools/typescript`, `agents`, `github`, `pnpm`, `prettier` and `tsdown`: the only remaining imports from a lower layer into a higher one, marked `@debt`. Every definer (`defineEslintConfig`, `defineTsdownConfig`, `defineViteConfig`, `defineVitestConfig`), the CLI plumbing and `bin/engrenage/*` call `loadConfig()` and receive the whole aggregate, so one invalid section blocks every tool. Other libraries (toolkit) need to read engrenage's understanding of a project without loading every tool.

Decided:

- **Layered registry.** No module imports a higher layer:
  - `project/config` loads raw input once per root (c12), memoized, and resolves the project sections lazily (global settings, structure, environments, presets, package, tsconfig, baseline, build). Its public `loadConfig(root)` exposes them.
  - each tool's `config/` role declares its section with the helper of #146; its definer (`operations/`) loads raw input through `project/config`, resolves its own section on demand and reads lower sections through `get`;
  - `workflows/registry` holds the full registry: every section, eager validation, the composed `Config` type, and the full `loadConfig(root)` that the CLI and `bin/` use.
- Consumers keep `export default defineTsdownConfig()`: no registry or resolver passed by hand.
- `get(section)` is lazy; a cycle between sections fails with its path. Sections are memoized per section and root.
- Unknown keys fail under eager validation (CLI, `--check`) and are ignored by a partial resolution.
- Environment sub-configs: a domain exports a sub-schema under its name (`tsconfig`, `exports`, `define`); each environment's schema is composed from these contributions.

## Prerequisites

- #146 is closed.

## Steps

1. Implement lazy section resolution with `get` in `project/config`, and replace the temporary `get` of #146.
2. Give each tool domain's definer its own section resolution through `project/config`, reading lower sections through `get`.
3. Add `workflows/registry` with eager validation and the composed `Config` type; switch the CLI plumbing and `bin/engrenage/*` to it.
4. Compose each environment's schema from the domains' sub-schemas.
5. Delete `createResolvedConfig`, `ResolvedConfig` and the `@debt` markers on the upward imports they caused.

## Tests

- A definer resolves while an unrelated section is invalid; eager validation of the same config fails on it, and on an unknown key.
- Two sections reading each other fail with the cycle path.

## Done when

No module imports a higher layer, `ResolvedConfig` no longer exists, each definer resolves only its sections, and the project's checks pass.
