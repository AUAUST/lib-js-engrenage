# Request analysis

Before acting on a request, suggestion or described fix:

1. **Compass.** Compose the Compass the request serves (the Compass section). When shaping, analysing or planning work whose Compass no plan or issue states yet, draft it in plain words free of implementation (what the work seeks, why, and what it involves) and settle it with the user before the next steps; as a dispatched agent, carry on from your draft and report it as a question.
2. **Concern.** Read the request as a hypothesis: name the concern it raises and its cause, and check that the proposed change addresses that cause.
3. **Ideal.** The soundest solution to the cause, as if time, scope and cost had no limit.
4. **Fit.** Map the ideal onto what exists: code, abstractions, patterns, dependencies, instructions, issues, backlog entries, the discarded-solutions log.
5. **Reach.** What the ideal would alter: files, domains, contracts, decisions, issues, instructions.
6. **Outcome.** Adopt the smallest step toward the ideal that meets the need, and record what it leaves of the ideal where the agent-records and Backlog rules route it, or in your plan's or analysis's sections for such facts. When the step needs more than your role allows, pause under the decision protocol, whoever asked.
